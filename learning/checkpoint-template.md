# Case study checkpoint worksheet

Use a personal copy to practice explaining the case study. These blank prompts are a reusable worksheet, not evidence of completed work or a competency assessment.

## Business and scope

- Explain what CaseCo sells and what the ISMS scope covers.
- Give two security responsibilities CaseCo retains when using cloud services.

## Risk reasoning

- Defend R01's likelihood and impact ratings.
- Explain R05's recovery risk and what evidence could support reassessment.
- Explain why R12 is accepted and what would invalidate that decision.

## Controls and treatment

- Justify permission review, privileged-access control and recoverable backups.
- Distinguish a planned target score from a reassessed current score.
- Explain why R02, R07 and R10 retain High target ratings.

## Evidence review

- Compare employment, approvals and before-snapshot access for AR027, AR004 and AR024.
- Explain why AR011 should be retained.
- Check the after snapshot and distinguish immediate corrections from systemic effectiveness.
- Describe what the synthetic snapshots cannot establish about a real system.

## Interview practice

- Give a two-minute project explanation.
- Walk through one business need, asset, risk, treatment, control and evidence chain.
- Defend one design decision and explain three project limitations.
