## Core vocabulary

**Confidentiality:** information is accessed only as authorized. **Integrity:** information and processing remain correct and protected from improper change. **Availability:** authorized users can obtain the service or information when needed.

**Asset owner:** accountable for the asset's business use and protection needs. **Risk owner:** accountable for deciding and managing the risk. They may be different roles.

**Current risk:** judgment at the assessment baseline considering assumed existing safeguards. **Target remaining risk:** a conditional estimate after proposed treatments work. **Acceptance:** an authorized, recorded decision, not an automatic result of a formula.

**Correction:** fixes a detected problem, such as disabling an old account. **Corrective action:** addresses why it happened and tests whether recurrence is prevented.

**SoA:** explains necessary controls and applicability decisions. This project's selected-control extract is incomplete by design; omitted controls have not been assessed.

## Suggested practice

Change R12's impact from 2 to 5 in a workbook copy. Observe the score/band, then explain why the old acceptance rationale would need review. Restore the baseline copy before publishing. Do not use spreadsheet calculations as a substitute for management judgment.
