# Interview rehearsal

Use these as worked answers to understand and adapt, not as claims that you have completed real client work.

## Two-minute structure

1. Describe the fictional business and its enterprise-customer security questions.
2. Explain why the scope includes development, cloud operation, information and supplier interfaces.
3. Describe the 10 assets and 12 risks, and explain one rating and its treatment.
4. Walk through AR027, CA01 and the revised disabled account record.
5. Explain that the three corrected samples do not prove recurring effectiveness and that the SoA is partial.

## Questions and worked reasoning

**Why did you choose a 5-by-5 matrix?** It is small enough to explain and apply consistently in this beginner case. I documented meanings, a planning horizon and authority rules before scoring. It is qualitative and not mandated by ISO. A real company could use another suitable method.

**What makes R05 Critical?** Backups are assumed to run, but no restore has been demonstrated. Likelihood 4 reflects the significant recoverability uncertainty. Impact 5 reflects prolonged service loss or unrecoverable ticket data. I would challenge these judgments with restore evidence and validated business recovery needs.

**Why keep an administrator?** AR011 belongs to an active platform employee with valid independent CEO approval for production administration. AR004 only has approval for Developer. Business need and valid authorization explain different decisions despite both roles appearing powerful.

**Did you reduce risk by correcting three accounts?** I verified three corrections in synthetic records. I did not measure real risk reduction. Recurring leaver and role-change processes, technical checks and later effectiveness review are still needed before applying the estimated target scores.

**Why is the SoA incomplete?** The first project deliberately covers selected implementation decisions. Its 21 rows do not decide applicability for the remaining reference controls. A full implementation would compare all necessary controls to the entire Annex A set, justify exclusions and include additional needed controls.

**How would this become a real engagement?** Validate the company facts, actual requirements and authorized scope; agree evidence access and responsibilities; verify against the full standards; assess actual risks; obtain decisions; implement and test controls; establish ongoing evaluation and improvement.

## Challenge questions

- If the marketing website adds customer login, would you retain R12's acceptance?
- If a supplier says it is certified, what evidence and customer-side responsibilities still need review?
- If no releases occur in a month, is OBJ03 100% achieved?
- If a disabled account retains a valid session, what does a CSV-only check miss?
- If you designed the ISMS, how would you arrange an objective internal audit?

## Readiness rule

You are ready to use the CV draft when you can explain scope, three ratings, three control choices and the limits without copying the text. Record your own reasoning and any changes you make. AI assistance with drafting does not establish your personal competence; that comes from understanding and practice.
