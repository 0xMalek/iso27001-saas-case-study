# Proposed 90-day implementation roadmap

Document ROADMAP-01 · Version 1.0 · Day 0 = 7 September 2026

Days are elapsed calendar days from Day 0. Day 30 is 2026-10-07, Day 60 is 2026-11-06, and Day 90 is 2026-12-06. These are fictional implementation targets, not claims that the work has happened.

| Phase | Priority actions | Owners | Evidence and decision gate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Day 0 | Confirm scope, policy direction, appetite and capacity; escalate High/Critical risks and decide immediate containment | CEO and all risk owners | Recorded priorities, resources and any time-limited risk decisions; currently pending |
| Days 1-30 | T01/T03 access process by day 14; T02 privileged review, T05 restore and T10 credential work by day 30 | Support, Platform and Engineering | Verified access workflow, MFA/privilege records, timed restore, secrets handling evidence; none implied by sample corrections |
| Days 31-60 | T04 devices, T08 suppliers and T09 incident work by day 45; T06 resilience, T07 release controls and T11 people records by day 60 | Platform, Engineering, People and Operations | Device evidence, supplier decisions, incident tabletop, recovery runbook, protected releases and people-folder review |
| Days 61-90 | Repeat control sampling; evaluate objectives; review estimated targets; revisit R12 acceptance; plan broader SoA and management-system work | CEO, owners and reviewer | Evidence-based reassessment, remaining-risk decisions, full first-month release metric and next-stage plan |

## Dependencies and resourcing

Scope and business requirements drive assets and risks. Accepted risk criteria precede treatment decisions. Approved roles and complete exports precede an operational access review. Recovery targets precede restore acceptance. Supplier responsibilities inform cloud recovery and exit planning. Release requirements precede measurement of release-control coverage.

Proposed capacity for management consideration: Platform 2 person-days/week, Engineering 1, People and Operations 0.5, Support 0.5, with a CEO checkpoint every two weeks. These are planning assumptions, not approved staffing or measured effort. If capacity is insufficient, the CEO reprioritizes and records exposure; due dates must not be silently changed.

No paid infrastructure is required to produce this portfolio. A real implementation would need costed technical designs and owner approval, which are outside this simulation.

## Day-90 management questions

- Are evidence populations complete and do repeat tests support operation over time?
- Have the three objectives been measured against their stated denominators and limits?
- Do R02, R07 and R10 still require additional treatment or formal high-risk acceptance?
- Are obligations, supplier responsibilities and all Annex A applicability decisions verified?
- Is the organization ready to plan a competent, objective internal audit and a complete management review?

This roadmap does not promise certification in 90 days. The follow-up audit and consulting portfolio projects will be separately designed.
