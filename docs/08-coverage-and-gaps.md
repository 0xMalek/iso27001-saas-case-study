# Management-system coverage and remaining gaps

Document COVERAGE-01 · Version 1.0 · Educational mapping, not a conformity assessment

The project proposes a scope for a real-world style ISMS but implements only selected portfolio activities. The following map covers management-system areas at a high level. It is not a clause-by-clause checklist or a conclusion that any requirement is fully satisfied.

| Area | Work in this release | Remaining implementation and evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clause 4: organizational context | Company scenario, interested parties, boundaries and climate-relevance judgment | Validate actual context, obligations, dependencies and full ISMS processes |
| Clause 5: leadership | Draft policy and responsibility assignments | Actual leadership commitment, approval, resources and communication |
| Clause 6: planning | Risk method, 12-risk assessment, treatments, selected-control extract and three objectives | Risk-owner approvals, complete Annex A comparison/SoA, further necessary controls and verified objective plans |
| Clause 7: support | Proposed role capacity, learning materials and document version convention | Competence evidence, staff awareness, communication and operating document-control process |
| Clause 8: operation | One synthetic access review with preserved evidence | Implementation of the wider treatment plan and sustained operating records; full operational planning |
| Clause 9: performance evaluation | Defined measures and proposed review checkpoints | Actual monitoring, competent objective internal audit, complete management review and required records |
| Clause 10: improvement | Three sample corrections, cause hypotheses and preventive actions | Implement systemic corrective actions, test effectiveness and demonstrate continual improvement |

## Evidence categories

- **Scenario assumption:** assumed business fact or safeguard; not independently verified.
- **Draft designed:** a populated planning artifact exists.
- **Demonstrated in sample:** a decision or correction is evidenced only in synthetic files.
- **Planned:** work or operating evidence does not yet exist.
- **Accepted in simulation:** a fictional owner decision is recorded, not a real approval.

The access-review follow-up verifies corrections in the fixture. It does not close the systemic corrective actions or establish sustained effectiveness. A future self-performed audit exercise must also be labelled as a simulation; auditing one's own design does not provide independent assurance.
